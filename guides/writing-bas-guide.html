Build a Sentence — Task Overview
  • Given a statement or question + several words out of order
  • Arrange them into one correct sentence
  • Some words may be extra and not used
  • Test day: 10 sentences · 5 min 50 sec · ~35 sec per question

7 Common Sentence Structures

1. Embedded Question
A question hidden inside a statement.

Summary: This task tests your ability to recognize sentence structure quickly under time pressure. Focus on identifying the grammar pattern first, then place the key verb and subject, and use the remaining words to complete the structure. Words that don't fit are decoys — ignore them.
