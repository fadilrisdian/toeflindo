Present Perfect vs Past Simple & Past Perfect

Practice the differences through active tasks

1. Quick Start

Fill in the blank (choose the correct sentence).

_____ (you / ever / be) to Paris?

Core Rule

Use the present perfect (have/has + past participle) for actions that have relevance to the present, experiences, or actions that occurred in an unfinished time period (e.g., this week, ever, already). Use the past simple for actions that happened at a specific finished time in the past (e.g., yesterday, last week, 1999). The past perfect (had + past participle) describes an action that happened before another past event.

2. Decision Model

When you see a time expression, decide which tense to use.

  • If the time is unspecified, up to now, or includes the present → Present Perfect
  • If the time is specific and finished → Past Simple
  • If you are talking about an event that happened before another past event → Past Perfect
