Present Simple vs Present Continuous

1. Quick Start

Fill in the blank with the correct form of the verb.

She _____ (read) a book now.

Core Rule

Use the present continuous for actions happening right now (e.g., She is reading). Use the present simple for habits, facts, or states, especially with stative verbs (e.g., I know).

2. Decision Helper
