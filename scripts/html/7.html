Present Perfect Mastery

1. Jump In!

Complete the sentence.

Tom can't find his key. Tom ___ his key.

Core Rule

We use have/has + past participle (the present perfect) to talk about a past action that has relevance now.

2. Decision Model

When should you use the present perfect?

  • ✅ The action started in the past and is still relevant or continues now.
  • ❌ The action is finished and the time is specific (e.g., yesterday).

Remember: have/has + past participle.

3. Key Contrasts

Which is correct?

“She has ___ to Paris.”

Choose the right adverb.

“I ___ finished my homework.”

4. Common Mistakes

Correct the sentence:

“Tom has lose his key.”

Correct the sentence:

“She has been to the store yesterday.”
