Present Perfect – Mastery Lesson

1. Immediate Start

Fill in the blank with the correct present perfect form.

Tom started reading a book two hours ago. He is still reading it.

Core Rule

We use the present perfect continuous (have/has been + -ing) for actions that started in the past and are still happening now, especially with “how long” or duration expressions.

2. Decision Rule

Choose the correct form for each situation.

She began learning French three years ago. She still studies it.

We know each other for a long time.

3. Key Contrasts

Decide whether to use the simple or continuous form.

How long ____ you ____ (wait) for the bus?

She ____ (visit) six countries so far.

It ____ (rain) all morning.

4. Common Mistakes

Correct the sentence.

Wrong: I have been known her for years.

Wrong: She has been lived here since 2010.

5. Mixed Practice

Apply what you’ve learned.

How long ____ you ____ (work) here?

He ____ (win) the championship four times.

She ____ (travel) around Europe for three months.

They ____ (make) five films since they left college.

6. Final Quiz (No Hints)

Answer the following without hints.

How long ____ you ____ (study) English?

She ____ (visit) six countries so far.

It ____ (rain) all morning.

He ____ (win) the championship four times.
