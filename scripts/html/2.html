Present Simple vs Present Continuous

Choose the right tense

1️⃣ Immediate Start

She ___ (watch) TV now.

2️⃣ Core Decision Rule

When do you use the Present Continuous?

  • Action happening now or around now.
  • Temporary situation.
  • Future plans with “going to” or scheduled events.

Use Present Simple for:

  • Habits, routines, general truths.
  • Permanent situations.
  • Scheduled future events (e.g., “The train leaves at 6.”)

3️⃣ Key Contrasts

I ___ (go) to school every day.

Right now, they ___ (play) chess.

4️⃣ Common Mistakes

She go to the gym every week. (Fix the verb)

They are eating breakfast at 8am every day. (Fix the tense)
