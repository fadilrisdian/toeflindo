Since / For & Present Perfect Practice
Master the use of “since”, “for”, and the present perfect

1. Immediate start

Fill the blank with since or for:

It’s been raining ___ lunchtime.

2. Core decision rule

When do we use since and when do we use for with the present perfect?

  • Use since with a specific point in time (e.g., Monday, 2010, when I arrived).
  • Use for with a length of time (e.g., three days, five years).
