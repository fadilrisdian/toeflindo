Present Continuous Practice

Start

Fill in the blank with the correct present continuous form.

She ___ (take) a picture.

Rule

The present continuous is formed with am / is / are + verb‑ing. Use it for actions happening right now, temporary situations, or planned future events.

When to use Present Continuous?

Answer the three questions. If you get YES to any, use the present continuous.

  1. Is the action happening right now?
  2. Is the situation temporary?
  3. Is it a future plan?

Use present continuous when:

  • Action is occurring at the moment of speaking.
  • Situation is temporary or changing.
  • Future arrangement that is already decided.

Key Contrasts

Which sentence uses the present continuous?

Choose the correct form.

He ___ (play) football now.

Common Mistakes

Correct the sentence.

She not (watch) TV.

Correct the sentence.

They (go) to school tomorrow.
