Present Perfect vs Past Simple

Quick Check

Choose the correct tense for the sentence.

1. I ____ (lose) my keys ___ this morning.



Core Rule

Use the present perfect for actions that have relevance to the present or for an unspecified time period that continues up to now (e.g., “this morning”, “today”, “already”). Use the past simple for actions that happened at a finished, specific time in the past (e.g., “yesterday”, “last year”).

Decision Guide

Match the time expression to the correct tense.

Time Expressions

  • today
  • since 2010
  • yesterday
  • last year
  • two weeks ago

Choose Tense

Present Perfect → Unfinished/unspecified time, continues to now.

Past Simple → Finished, specific past time.
