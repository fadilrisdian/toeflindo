2️⃣ Core Decision Rule

When do we use present perfect for “how long” questions?

  1. Is the action still happening now?
  2. Do we talk about the length of time?

Use present perfect (simple or continuous) when the action started in the past and continues up to now, and the question asks about the length of time (e.g., “How long have you lived here?”). Use simple present for habits or states that are always true.
