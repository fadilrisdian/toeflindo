Present Perfect Continuous

Practice the form, use, and contrast

1. Immediate Start

Fill in the blank with the correct question.

Hi Kate, (you / swim?)

2. Core Decision Rule

When do you use the Present Perfect Continuous?

Is the activity still happening now?

Rule: Use have/has been + –ing when the action started in the past and is still continuing.

3. Key Contrasts

Choose the correct form.

“I ___ (read) that book for two hours.”

“She ___ (paint) the bedroom.”

“They ___ (play) tennis since 2 o’clock.”

4. Common Mistakes

Correct the sentence.

I’m learning Japanese. I started classes in December.

She works here. She started three years ago.

5. Mixed Practice

(you / wait / long?)

(I / think) about what you said and I’ve decided to take your advice.

“She ___ (teach) for ten years.”

Answer the following without hints.
