Present Perfect & Continuous

Master using “ever”, “never”, and “have/has been + -ing”.

1️⃣ Try it first

Fill the blanks. Choose the correct words.

____ you ever a horse?

Rule

Use have/has + past participle for experiences up to now. Ever is used in questions, never in negatives.

2️⃣ Decision guide

When to use each form.

Present Perfect

  • have/has + past participle
  • Used for experiences, actions with result now
  • Often with ever/never, for/since (duration)

Present Perfect Continuous

  • have/has been + verb‑ing
  • Action started in past and continues or just stopped
  • Often with for or since

3️⃣ Quick contrasts

Select the correct word.

I been working for two hours.

She ___ ever visited Paris?

They ___ never seen a tiger.

4️⃣ Fix the mistake

Rewrite the sentence correctly.

Incorrect: I have ate breakfast.

Correct: I have

Incorrect: She has been to the gym yesterday.

Correct: She
